# 🧠 Neuroplasticity: How Habits Form in the Brain

An interactive browser game that shows what happens in your brain when you build (or break) a habit.

**▶ Play it here:** https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/YOUR-REPO/

## What it is

Drag 5 colored brain regions into a brain and let a person do everyday activities. Every time an activity runs, signals fire through the network, and the connections it uses get thicker. That is neuroplasticity: *neurons that fire together wire together.*

**The 5 brain regions**

| Color | Region | Role |
|---|---|---|
| 🔵 | Prefrontal cortex | Planning, conscious control |
| 🟡 | Reward system (striatum) | Dopamine, habit autopilot |
| 🟢 | Hippocampus | Learning, memory |
| 🔴 | Motor cortex | Movement |
| 🟣 | Amygdala | Emotion, stress, social signals |

**The 5 activities:** 🏃 running · 📱 scrolling · 🗣️ learning a language · 🧘 meditating · 🤝 meeting friends

## How to win

Before **day 100**, get **3 of the 4 good habits** to at least 80% automaticity and keep the **scrolling autopilot under 50%**. You earn up to 3 stars.

Along the way you'll run into:

- **Energy:** New habits are effortful and get cheaper with every repetition.
- **Free time:** You get 5 hours a day. Staying up late costs you sleep.
- **Sleep:** Half of each day's progress only counts after a good night's sleep (consolidation).
- **Cues:** Linking a habit to a fixed trigger ("after breakfast") makes it form faster.
- **Phone urges:** You either resist (costs energy, weakens the scroll loop) or give in.
- **Random days:** Sick days and stressful days.

With good play, you win around day 65, close to the 66-day average from the research.

## The science behind it

- **Hebbian learning and LTP:** Synapses that are used together get stronger.
- **Myelination:** Pathways you use often get faster and more reliable.
- **From PFC to striatum:** Control shifts from effortful conscious thinking to an autopilot.
- **Habit loop:** cue → routine → reward, driven by dopamine.
- **Sleep consolidation:** Learning is locked in at night.
- **Synaptic pruning:** Unused paths weaken, but they never fully disappear (which is why relapses happen).
- **Lally et al. (2009):** It took a median of **66 days** for a new behavior to become automatic, with a range of 18 to 254 days.

> This is a simplified learning model. A real brain has about 86 billion neurons, and each orb here stands for an entire region. "Energy", learning speeds and the meditation bonus are game simplifications. The idea of willpower as a depletable resource ("ego depletion") is debated in research.

## Tech

One self-contained `index.html` file using plain HTML, CSS, SVG and JavaScript. There are no frameworks and no build step. Open it in any browser, or host it on GitHub Pages.
